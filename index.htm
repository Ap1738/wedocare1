Tailored Nutrition Plans

Designed by dietary specialists for distinct lifestyle needs.

For infants

Focuses on whole foods, lean proteins, and complex carbohydrates for sustained energy throughout the day.

  • ✓ High-fiber nutrient density
  • Rich in healthy monounsaturated fats, potassium, and fiber for brain development. Serve as a smooth mash or soft slices.
  • ✓ Moderate carbohydrate distribution
  • Starchy vegetables: cooked and mashed sweet potatoes, carrots, butternut squash, or regular potatoes.
  • ✓ Heart-healthy unsaturated fats
  • Fish: soft-flaked, low-mercury fish like salmon provides crucial omega-3 fatty acids.
  • Legumes and eggs: mashed beans, lentils, tofu, and well-cooked eggs offer plant-based and animal proteins.
Popular

Low-Carb

Optimized for glycemic control and metabolic efficiency by substituting carbohydrates with healthy fats.

  • ✓ Controlled insulin response
  • ✓ Rich in healthy lipids & avocado
  • ✓ Essential micronutrient coverage
    • Key Categories of Low-Carb Foods Proteins: Unprocessed meats (beef, lamb, pork, chicken), fish and seafood (salmon, trout, sardines, tuna), and eggs. These contain virtually zero carbs. Non-Starchy Vegetables: Leafy greens (spinach, kale, lettuce), broccoli, cauliflower, zucchini, cucumbers, bell peppers, and asparagus. Healthy Fats and Oils: Extra virgin olive oil, avocado oil, coconut oil, and butter. Dairy Products: Full-fat cheese, Greek yogurt, heavy cream, and butter. (Opt for unsweetened options to avoid added sugars).

Plant-Based Power

Complete vegan nutritional profile utilizing legumes, grains, seeds, and nuts for optimal protein synthesis.

  • ✓ 100% Plant-derived nutrients
  • ✓ Rich in natural antioxidants
  • ✓ Complete amino acid spectrum
    • Complementary Amino Acid Profiles Legumes (Lentils, chickpeas, black beans, edamame) High in: Lysine, Threonine Low in: Methionine, Cysteine Grains (Quinoa, brown rice, oats, farro, buckwheat) High in: Methionine, Cysteine Low in: Lysine Seeds (Hemp seeds, chia seeds, pumpkin seeds, flaxseeds) High in: Methionine, Cysteine, Healthy Fats (Omega-3/6) Low in: Lysine (varies by seed type) Nuts (Almonds, walnuts, cashews, Brazil nuts) High in: Healthy unsaturated fats, Vitamin E, Methionine Low in: Lysine
